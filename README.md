## Task 1: AI Website Copy Generator for Local Businesses

### Project Overview

This project was completed as part of the Future Interns Prompt Engineering Internship.

The objective of this task was to create website-ready copy for a local business using structured AI prompts.

### Business Details

- **Business Name:** Coastal Comfort Hotel
- **Business Type:** Hotel
- **Location:** Mangaluru, Karnataka
- **Target Audience:** Tourists, families, couples, and business travelers
- **AI Tool Used:** Claude

### Project Objective

The goal was to generate clear, simple, persuasive, and website-ready content for a fictional local hotel.

The generated website copy includes:

1. Homepage Content
2. Services Page Content
3. Call-to-Action (CTA) Content

### Prompt Engineering Approach

Structured prompts were used to guide the AI model.

Each prompt provided relevant business information, target audience, business goals, required content sections, tone, and restrictions on unsupported claims.

The prompts were designed to produce realistic content rather than generic marketing copy.

### Deliverables

#### 1. Homepage

A complete homepage copy was generated for Coastal Comfort Hotel, including a clear value proposition and content suitable for the hotel's target audience.

#### 2. Services Page

A services page was generated with descriptions tailored to the hotel business.

#### 3. CTA Content

Five CTA variations were generated:

- Direct Room Booking CTA
- Enquiry CTA
- Family Stay CTA
- Business Traveler CTA
- General Hotel Stay CTA

### Key Prompting Principles Used

- Clear role definition for the AI
- Detailed business context
- Defined target audience
- Specific content requirements
- Appropriate tone and style
- Conversion-focused messaging
- Restrictions against unsupported claims
- Website-ready formatting

### AI Tool

**Claude**

The Future Interns Prompt Engineering task allows the use of AI tools such as ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini.

### Outcome

The final output is a complete AI-generated website copy set consisting of Homepage, Services, and CTA content for Coastal Comfort Hotel.

The project demonstrates the use of structured prompting for local-business website copywriting and conversion-focused messaging.

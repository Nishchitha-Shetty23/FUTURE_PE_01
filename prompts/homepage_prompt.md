# Homepage Prompt

## Role
Act as an expert website copywriter for a local hotel.

## Business Information
- Business Name: Coastal Comfort Hotel
- Location: Mangaluru, Karnataka
- Business Type: Hotel

## Target Audience
- Tourists
- Families
- Couples
- Business travelers

## Business Goal
Create engaging, clear, and website-ready homepage copy that introduces the hotel and encourages visitors to consider a booking stay.

## Requirements
Create homepage content that includes:
- A clear headline/value proposition
- A welcoming introduction
- Information that speaks to the target audience
- A section highlighting the hotel's overall stay experience
- A clear call to action

## Tone
- Warm
- Professional
- Welcoming
- Trustworthy
- Simple and easy to understand

## Restrictions
- Do not invent prices, discounts, awards, ratings, facilities, or guarantees.
- Do not make unsupported claims such as "best hotel in Mangaluru."
- Keep the content realistic for a fictional hotel.
- Avoid excessive marketing language.
- Make the content suitable for a professional hotel website.

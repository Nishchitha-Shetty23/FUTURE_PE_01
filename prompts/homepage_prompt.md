# Homepage Prompt

## Role
Act as an expert website copywriter creating clear, trustworthy, conversion-focused homepage content for a local hotel.

## Business Information
- Business Name: Coastal Comfort Hotel
- Location: Mangaluru, Karnataka
- Business Type: Hotel

## Target Audience
- Tourists
- Families
- Couples
- Business travelers

## Business Goal
Create professional homepage copy that introduces Coastal Comfort Hotel, explains the overall stay experience, highlights the hotel's genuine features and benefits, and encourages visitors to consider booking or making an enquiry.

## Requirements

Create homepage content using exactly this structure:

### 1. Headline
Write one clear and memorable headline that communicates the hotel's main value proposition and location.

### 2. Subheadline
Write one concise sentence summarizing the hotel's overall appeal for visitors.

### 3. Welcome to Coastal Comfort Hotel
Write 2–3 short paragraphs that:
- Introduce the hotel and its location in Mangaluru.
- Explain that the hotel is suitable for different types of travelers.
- Describe the overall stay experience.
- Avoid repeating the headline or subheadline.

### 4. Our Stay Experience
Create exactly 3 short sections:

#### Comfortable Stay
Explain the focus on comfortable, well-maintained accommodation.

#### Welcoming Experience
Explain the focus on helpful, attentive, and welcoming guest service.

#### Convenient Stay
Explain the practical convenience of staying at the hotel in Mangaluru.

### 5. Why Choose Coastal Comfort Hotel?
Create exactly 4 distinct points:

#### Convenient Location
Explain the benefit of staying in Mangaluru with convenient access to different parts of the city.

#### Comfortable Accommodation
Explain the availability of comfortable and well-maintained rooms.

#### Guest-Focused Service
Explain the hotel's helpful and attentive approach to guests.

#### Suitable for Different Travelers
Mention that the hotel welcomes tourists, families, couples, and business travelers.

### 6. Book Your Stay Today
Create a final booking-focused section containing:

#### Heading
One short, inviting heading encouraging visitors to plan their stay.

#### Supporting Text
2 concise sentences encouraging visitors to enquire about room availability and their stay.

#### CTA
Use these two button labels exactly:
- Book Now
- Enquire Today

## Content Guidelines
- Keep the homepage concise and easy to scan.
- Make every section provide new information.
- Avoid unnecessary repetition.
- Keep the writing natural and suitable for a real hotel website.
- Use simple, professional English.
- Make headings distinct from one another.
- Keep paragraphs short.

## Tone
- Warm
- Professional
- Welcoming
- Trustworthy
- Clear
- Natural
- Not overly promotional

## Restrictions
- Do not invent prices, discounts, offers, awards, ratings, reviews, room types, facilities, services, or guarantees.
- Do not claim the hotel is the best, cheapest, most luxurious, highest-rated, or similar.
- Do not invent exact distances or travel times to attractions or other locations.
- Do not add facilities that have not been provided.
- Do not use exaggerated marketing claims.
- Do not repeat the same information across multiple sections.
- Do not add extra sections beyond the structure requested above.
- Do not add explanations before or after the homepage copy.
- Follow the exact structure requested.

# Services Page Prompt

## Role
Act as an expert website copywriter for a local hotel.

## Business Information
- Business Name: Coastal Comfort Hotel
- Location: Mangaluru, Karnataka
- Business Type: Hotel

## Target Audience
- Tourists
- Families
- Couples
- Business travelers

## Business Goal
Create clear and appealing services page content that helps visitors understand the hotel's services and encourages them to consider staying at the hotel.

## Requirements
Create a services page with descriptions relevant to a hotel.

For each service:
- Provide a clear service name
- Write a short and useful description
- Explain the value of the service to the visitor
- Keep the information realistic for a fictional hotel

## Tone
- Warm
- Professional
- Welcoming
- Trustworthy
- Simple and easy to understand

## Restrictions
- Do not invent prices, discounts, awards, ratings, facilities, or guarantees.
- Do not make unsupported claims.
- Do not claim the hotel provides a service unless it is reasonable for the fictional business.
- Avoid excessive marketing language.
- Keep the content suitable for a professional hotel website.
